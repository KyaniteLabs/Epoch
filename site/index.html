The Problem

AI agents hallucinate timelines

They're incredibly powerful. They write code, debug systems, automate workflows. But ask "how long will this take?" and they guess — badly.

Without Epoch

Your AI confidently says "this will take 2 hours." Two days later, you're still waiting.

  • AI agents estimate in token budgets, not minutes
  • Events 10 tokens apart have identical encodings whether 1 second or 1 hour apart
  • LLMs replicate and amplify human optimism bias through agentic overconfidence
With Epoch

Epoch gives AI grounded estimates using 40+ years of software engineering research, packaged into 24 callable tools.

  • Structured external tools — not prompt hacks
  • 24 MCP tools covering time, dates, estimation, analytics, and cost
  • Works with Claude, Cursor, VS Code, and any MCP client

Research-backed approach

A UPenn negotiation study showed 4% deal closure under wall-clock time limits vs 99% under turn-based limits. Injecting explicit time state into prompts produced an 8x improvement. Epoch makes time legible to LLMs through structured external tools.
